    A small gallery with a long view

    Kalakriti began in 2025 as a single room in Vijayawada and a simple idea: that good contemporary work should be easy to see, easy to understand and easy to live with.

    We represent a small group of artists working in painting, photography and digital media. Each work is shown at a generous size with the story behind it, and every sale includes a signed certificate of authenticity and insured delivery.

    Collectors are welcome to ask questions, request more images, or arrange a private viewing. We would rather you take your time.

    A picture should be allowed to be quiet.
